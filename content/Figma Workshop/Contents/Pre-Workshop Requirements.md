

## Mandatory Prerequisites

To ensure a productive session, all participants must satisfy the following requirements prior to the start of the workshop:

### 1. Hardware and Connectivity

- **Laptops:** Participants must bring a personal laptop or have confirmed access to a computer station for the duration of the event.
    
- **Internet Access:** Ensure your device is configured for campus Wi-Fi.
    

### 2. Account Configuration

- **College Email Access:** You must have active access to your official college email. This is required for the Education Plan verification process.
    
- **Figma for Education:** Figma provides a professional-tier plan to students at no cost. You are required to register via the link below:
    
    - [Verify Education Status](https://www.figma.com/education/)
        

### 3. Communication

- **WhatsApp Group:** Upon arrival at the venue, please join the dedicated workshop WhatsApp group as directed by the Design Team. This will be the primary channel for sharing assets, links, and real-time troubleshooting.
