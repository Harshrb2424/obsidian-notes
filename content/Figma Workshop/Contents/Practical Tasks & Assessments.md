
## Guided Tasks

Practical application is the most effective way to retain design concepts. Please complete the tasks in the order listed below.

### Primary Task: Foundation Building

This task is mandatory for all participants and focuses on the core mechanics of the software.

- [Task 1: Figma Basics](https://www.figma.com/design/OGa3aaTRJtxDVlT1q6OHuT/Figma-basics?node-id=1669-162202&t=MNDXqg5urViwsOLL-1)
    

### Secondary Task: Ideation (Time Permitting)

For students who finish the primary task early, we will explore FigJam—Figma’s collaborative whiteboarding tool—to understand the ideation phase of design.

- [Task 2: FigJam Basics](https://www.figma.com/design/OGa3aaTRJtxDVlT1q6OHuT/Figma-basics?node-id=1669-162202&t=MNDXqg5urViwsOLL-1)
