## Plexus Design Club: Figma Fundamentals Workshop

Welcome to the official documentation for the Plexus Design Club Figma Workshop. This resource is designed to guide you through the initial setup, the live workshop session, and your continued learning journey in interface design.

### Workshop Objectives

- Master the fundamental tools and navigation within the Figma interface.
    
- Understand the principles of collaborative design.
    
- Successfully complete the "Figma Basics" practical assessment.
    
- Establish a foundation for advanced UX/UI design workflows.
