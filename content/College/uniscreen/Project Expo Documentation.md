# **UniScreen**

### _The Operating System for Modern University Campuses_

**[Live Demo: uniscreen.vercel.app](https://uniscreen.vercel.app/)**

---

## **Overview**

**UniScreen** is a comprehensive Smart Screen Management System designed to revolutionize real-time information distribution across campus. From managing digital displays to scheduling critical announcements, UniScreen keeps students, faculty, and staff synchronized through a unified, intelligent ecosystem.

---

## **Core Platform Features**

Built to handle the complex communication demands of modern educational institutions, UniScreen offers a robust suite of administrative and pedagogical tools:

### **1. Centralized Command**

Manage every screen on campus through a "single pane of glass." Instantly push updates to cafeterias, labs, and hallways simultaneously from one central dashboard.

### **2. Smart Scheduling**

"Set it and forget it." Plan and schedule content weeks in advance with built-in auto-expiration features to ensure information remains current and relevant.

### **3. Emergency Override**

Safety first. The system features a high-priority override for instant, campus-wide alerts, ensuring critical safety notifications reach every corner of the institution in seconds.

### **4. Analytics & Insights**

Monitor your network’s performance with detailed reports on screen uptime, content engagement, and device health to ensure your message is always seen.

### **5. Role-Based Security (RBAC)**

Secure and scalable. Granular permissions ensure that students, faculty, and administrators access only the tools and data relevant to their roles.

---

## **Designed for Every Corner of Campus**

UniScreen adapts its interface and functionality based on the physical environment:

### **Classroom Mode**

- **Dynamic Course Details:** Displays current and upcoming lecture information.
    
- **Instant Notifications:** Real-time alerts regarding room changes or faculty updates.
    
- **Syllabus Integration:** Access to AI-generated viva questions and academic resources.
